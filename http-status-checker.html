HTTP check results

Response details: Status, response headers and request latency are captured for each redirect hop and the final response. The optional body preview makes a separate, bounded GET request for the selected hop and reads at most 8 KB of text content.
Limitations and safety: Checks publicly reachable HTTP/HTTPS URLs on standard ports 80 and 443. Private, local and reserved targets are blocked. Cloudflare's network location, bot protection, firewalls, geoblocking and server rules can produce results different from a browser or Googlebot. Body previews are text-only and limited to 8 KB; binary bodies are not displayed.

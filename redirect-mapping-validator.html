CSV analysis · No account required
Expected CSV format

Use a header row with source and target. Both columns should contain absolute URLs, including http:// or https://.

source,target https://example.com/old,https://example.com/new https://example.com/legacy,https://example.com/current
Checks are performed in your browser. Your CSV is not transmitted.
